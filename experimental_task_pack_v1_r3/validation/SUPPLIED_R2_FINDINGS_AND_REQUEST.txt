R2-NEW-001 — MIXED-CLAUSE HYPOTHETICAL BYPASS
============================================================

CRITICAL RELEASE BLOCKER.

The Round 2 parser classifies an entire sentence as hypothetical
when it begins with "If" and contains modal language.

It then skips further authority checks.

But one sentence can contain both:

A hypothetical proposition

AND

an independent factual assertion.

A hypothetical opening cannot grant immunity to a factual
claim later in the sentence.
