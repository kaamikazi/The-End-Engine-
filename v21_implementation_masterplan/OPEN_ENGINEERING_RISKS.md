## Scope boundaries that cannot be silently crossed

Protected Engine/Harness/evaluator, A/B/C algorithms/prompts/public tasks/settings/caps4/5/5, original three-cycle behavior and historical evidence remain untouched. F01 ownership/fences/exclusive publication/pins and F02 independent BLOCKED rejection remain unchanged. F03 admission and F04 numeric semantics cannot be modified to accommodate the new package. Shared Ledger/Gateway/native code and current research constructors are existing cross-project dependencies; initial strategy drains them, does not edit them. Any future need to alter them requires an exact separate authorization request and source compatibility review. No current planning risk confers that authorization.

## Boundedness clarification

Controller admission/drain coordination is itself bounded: propose at most5s per coordination wait within the same <=2 acquisition-attempt policy, releasing what was acquired on failure. Never wait for a mutator under L1/L2. This operational bound cannot bypass the approved <=5s per guard wait or create unlimited preflight retries. Parsing/capture/core verification duration is separate; resource/time caps must fail explicitly without a partially certified view. No observed in-epoch violation can be erased by a later automatically minted epoch.

Remaining scientific limits are not blockers to contract implementation: recorded identities/usage and candidate binding do not establish authentic inference/billing, answer correctness or model improvement. Scores stay NOT_COMPUTED and answer success NOT_ASSERTED. Authenticated human semantic/scientific adjudication and provider compatibility remain pending, outside this plan.
