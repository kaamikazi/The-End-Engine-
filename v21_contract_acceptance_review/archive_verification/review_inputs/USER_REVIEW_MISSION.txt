====================================================
PHASE 8 — REVIEW STOP RULE
====================================================

Distinguish:

A. MATERIAL CONTRACT DEFECT
   The specification is contradictory, unsafe,
   ambiguous or untestable.

B. IMPLEMENTATION PREREQUISITE
   The specification is adequate but requires future
   engineering before runtime claims can be made.

C. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATION
   The contract cannot establish empirical model
   performance or semantic answer correctness.

D. OPTIONAL FUTURE ENHANCEMENT
   Useful but not needed for contract acceptance.

Only A blocks contract approval automatically.

An unimplemented but precisely defined future
requirement is not itself a contract contradiction.

If a prerequisite is technically impossible using
the declared allowed scope, explain that and determine
whether it invalidates the design.

Do not invent endless new mandatory features.
