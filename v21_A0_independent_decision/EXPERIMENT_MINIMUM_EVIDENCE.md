## Three separate authority questions

1. **Existing core eligibility:** the accepted preservation decision keeps the independent completed-source verifier and BLOCKED rejection mandatory. It does not retroactively certify all historical runs or resolve the WIP suite failures. A source must actually pass its unchanged verification; a caller's `VERIFIED` field is insufficient.
2. **New v2.1 eligible view:** R3 and R1 acquisition step 9 require existing-source verification bound to the **same real A/S guarded membership/content cut**. An independently valid legacy artifact plus a later I observation cannot be combined into a v2.1 NORMAL or RESTRICTED view. I/X can support only a grounded noncertifying diagnostic (or an actual typed error), never a selected certified candidate. Verified persisted INTERRUPTED is restricted; uncertain COMPLETED is ineligible.
3. **New complete accounting:** A alone establishes a closed recorded identity graph, not actual provider inference or complete cost. COMPLETE totals additionally need VERIFIED independently eligible source, closed generation, every required raw/usage/settlement dimension, exact full coverage and no unknown/outstanding/conflicting cost. S may accompany a legacy-valid same-cut eligible source with partial accounting; it cannot establish COMPLETE. All claims retain full_run_certification_permitted=false.

These distinctions are why A-tier authority is unnecessary for each diagnostic field and why I/X is insufficient for the mission's complete-resource endpoint. A source-scoped known usage value may be scientifically useful as a retained observation. It cannot be relabeled a run total. A lower bound over known rows is not a measured total or an unbiased basis for comparing efficiency across conditions.

## Six-condition feasibility decision

| Condition | Actual prescribed evidence to retain | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| DIRECT | Actual single historical direct-answer exchange, any physical retries, actual delivered candidate | Evidence design is representable; no real six-condition experiment readiness demonstrated. Native durable failure/accounting coverage remains a gate. |
| SELF_REFLECT | Initial, critique and revision plus any physical protocol repair; critique is a charged call even though it is not an answer candidate | Same gate. Counting only two candidates omits a model call. Missing original initial answer cannot be recreated as C0. |
| FROZEN_CURRENT_END | All native Engine generation, judge/verification/comparison/branch/synthesis/repair exchanges actually made under the frozen configuration, trajectory-selected final | Same gate, with the broadest native purpose coverage. Candidate count and iteration count are not physical-call totals; model agreement is not external verification. |
| SPARSE_DELIBERATION | INITIAL, need gate, optional supported revision, and one permitted format-repair call if actually used | r3 durable witness chain is reusable. Original actual stages and branch decisions must establish condition fidelity; four is a bound, not observed usage. |
| DIRECTED_REPAIR | INITIAL, inventory, plan, optional revision and permitted format repair | Same distinction; charge rejected/failed control calls too. Five is a bound, not a measured total. |
| BOUNDED_ALTERNATIVES | Actual initial plus requirements/constraints candidates, selector and permitted format repair | Preserve every real candidate and selector cost. Byte diversity/model preference does not prove reasoning diversity or answer correctness. Five is a bound. |

The minimum design can represent all six conditions and their legitimate restricted/failed outcomes. **The currently evidenced I/X-only package cannot support the full planned six-condition inference-and-resource comparison.** It has no v2.1 eligible terminal authority, complete historical call coverage, measured actual inference, or complete real total cost. The current entire integration is explicitly OFFLINE_SYNTHETIC_RECORDING_ONLY; no observation adapter changes that.

For a future preregistered answer-quality endpoint, a separately independently eligible legacy source may remain useful even if a new diagnostic observer has I authority. Such use must preserve the legacy source boundary, original candidate and frozen evaluation; it is not v2.1 adapter certification or a complete-resource result. This review does not change the mission to that narrower experiment. Missing totals block all six-condition total-cost rankings, efficiency/quality-per-cost ratios and claims of equal resource use; report the endpoint INCOMPLETE with cell-specific reasons. Do not silently drop failed expensive cells or assign zero to them. Any prespecified outcome-only analysis must disclose missingness and selection effects and cannot claim the omitted cost endpoint passed.

## Minimality conclusion

The experiment needs complete evidence for the endpoints it claims; it does not need maximum authority for every retained diagnostic. Under the approved v2.1 contract, full resource totals require the stronger A predicate plus source/usage coverage, and new eligible views require A/S. Neither requirement proves that invasive production writer changes are the only possible implementation: a genuinely exhaustive operational admission/drain/exclusion profile may supply the cooperating domain, but the current two-worker model and A0-18 bypass do not establish it. The next bounded feasibility slice must settle that alternative and native evidence coverage before choosing production enrollment or shipping a diagnostic-only observer as the research solution.
